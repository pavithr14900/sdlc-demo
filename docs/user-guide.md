# User Guide

## Overview
This employee leave application is designed for employees within our organization to request, track, and manage their leave. It solves the problem of manual leave tracking and communication, providing a streamlined process for employees to submit leave requests and for managers to approve or deny them. Employees can view their leave balance, pending requests, and history, while managers can oversee all leave requests within their team.

## Getting Started
1. **Login**: Use your company credentials to log in to the application.
2. **Navigate to Leave Requests**: From the dashboard, click on the "Leave Requests" tab.
3. **Verify success**: You should see a list of your pending leave requests and your current leave balance.

## User Roles and Permissions

### Employee
- **Capabilities**: Submit leave requests, view leave balance, view pending requests, view leave history.
- **Typical workflows**:
  - Submit a leave request for vacation.
  - View the status of a submitted leave request.
  - Check the remaining leave balance before submitting a request.
- **Restrictions**: Cannot approve or deny leave requests, cannot view other employees' leave information.

### Manager
- **Capabilities**: View and approve/deny leave requests for team members, view team leave balances.
- **Typical workflows**:
  - Review a leave request submitted by a team member.
  - Approve or deny the leave request.
  - View the leave history of team members.
- **Restrictions**: Cannot submit leave requests on behalf of team members, cannot view leave information outside their team.

## Core Workflows

### Submit a Leave Request
This workflow is used to request leave from the system.

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to the "Leave Requests" section by clicking on the tab in the main menu.
2. Click the "Request Leave" button to open the leave request form.
3. Enter the "Start Date", "End Date", and "Reason" with values like:
   - `Start Date: 2023-10-01`
   - `End Date: 2023-10-05`
   - `Reason: Annual Vacation`
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit the request.
5. **Expected outcome**: A success message indicating that the leave request has been submitted.

**Common mistakes:**
- **Overlapping Dates**: Ensure the start and end dates do not overlap with existing leave requests. To avoid this, check your current leave balance and pending requests before submitting.
- **Incomplete Information**: Ensure all required fields are filled out. If the form is incomplete, you will be prompted to fill in the missing information.

### Approve/Deny a Leave Request
This workflow is used by managers to approve or deny leave requests submitted by employees.

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to the "Leave Requests" section by clicking on the tab in the main menu.
2. Click on the "Pending Requests" tab to view requests that require action.
3. Click on the "Review" button next to the leave request you wish to action.
4. Click the "Approve" or "Deny" button to take the appropriate action.
5. **Expected outcome**: The leave request status should update to "Approved" or "Denied".

**Tips:**
- **Check Leave Balance**: Before approving a request, ensure the employee has sufficient leave balance.
- **Provide Feedback**: When denying a request, provide a reason to help the employee understand the decision.

### View Leave History
This workflow is used by employees and managers to view past leave requests and their statuses.

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to the "Leave History" section by clicking on the tab in the main menu.
2. Use the filters to narrow down the results by date range or leave type.
3. **Expected outcome**: A list of past leave requests with their statuses.

**Tips:**
- **Export History**: Use the "Export" button to download a CSV file of your leave history for personal records.

## Data Management

### Creating Records
To create a new leave request, navigate to the "Leave Requests" section and click the "Request Leave" button. Fill in the required fields: "Start Date", "End Date", and "Reason". Ensure the dates do not overlap with existing leave requests. Click "Submit" to create the record.

### Editing Records
Editing leave requests is only possible before they are approved. Once approved, the request becomes read-only. To edit a pending request, navigate to the "Pending Requests" tab, click "Edit" next to the request, make the necessary changes, and click "Save".

### Deleting Records
Deleting leave requests is not supported as it may lead to data loss. Instead, requests can be denied if they are incorrect. If a request has been mistakenly approved, contact support to resolve the issue.

### Filtering and Search
Use the filters in the "Leave Requests" and "Leave History" sections to find specific records. You can filter by date range, leave type, and status. Use the search bar to find records by employee name or request ID.

## Reports and Exports
The application provides several reports, including leave balance reports and leave request summaries. To generate a report, navigate to the "Reports" section, select the desired report, and click "Generate". Reports can be exported in CSV format for further analysis.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I request leave on a half-day?**
A: Yes, you can request leave for a half-day by setting the start and end dates to the same day and specifying "Half Day" as the reason.

**Q: How can I check my leave balance?**
A: Your leave balance is displayed on the dashboard and in the "Leave Requests" section. It shows your remaining leave for the current year.

**Q: Can I request leave for a future date?**
A: Yes, you can request leave for a future date by entering the desired start and end dates in the leave request form.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **Unable to Submit Leave Request**: Ensure all required fields are filled out and the dates do not overlap with existing requests. If the problem persists, contact support.
- **Leave Request Status Stuck on Pending**: Check if the request was submitted correctly and if your manager has access to approve it. If the issue continues, contact support.
- **Incorrect Leave Balance**: Ensure all leave requests have been processed correctly. If the balance is still incorrect, contact support.

### Performance Tips
- **Use Filters**: Use the filters to narrow down the results and improve performance when viewing large lists of leave requests.
- **Regularly Check for Updates**: Ensure your application is up to date to benefit from performance improvements and bug fixes.

## Best Practices
- **Submit Requests Early**: Submit leave requests well in advance to allow time for approval.
- **Check Pending Requests**: Regularly check the "Pending Requests" tab to ensure your requests are processed in a timely manner.
- **Provide Detailed Reasons**: When submitting a leave request, provide a detailed reason to help your manager understand the need for leave.
